Evaluation methodology

  • Stratified train/validation/test split with the test set held out from model and threshold selection.
  • Threshold chosen on validation data under an explicit false-negative/false-positive cost model.
  • Three-way decision (hate / non-hate / needs review) with abstentions counted, not hidden.
  • Calibration reported as Brier score and expected calibration error.
  • Robustness measured on an author-constructed adversarial probe set, reported per category.
  • Explanation quality measured by comprehensiveness and sufficiency under masking.

Known limitations

    Responsible use

    Attribution highlights model-derived features. It does not establish intent, causation, or the truth of any claim about a person or group.
